For candidates
How do I apply for a job?
Browse open roles from the Jobs tab, open the one you're interested in, and click Apply. You can attach a resume specific to that application, or use the one already on your profile.
How does the match score work?
When you apply, your resume is compared against the job's stated requirements — skills, experience, education, and overall text similarity — to produce a percentage match score. It's a screening aid for recruiters, not a pass/fail judgment on you.
Can recruiters see my profile if I haven't applied anywhere?
Yes. Completing your profile makes you visible in the recruiter-side candidate directory, so HR users at any company can find and review you even before you apply to a specific role.
What resume file formats are supported?
PDF, DOCX, plain text, and common image formats (for scanned resumes, processed via OCR) are all supported.
For HR / recruiters
Why does an applicant show a 0% match with a screening error?
This means the resume file couldn't be parsed — usually an unsupported or corrupted file rather than a genuinely poor match. Check the flagged warning on the application page, then use "Rescreen resume" once the file issue is resolved.
What's the difference between "My Jobs" and "Candidates"?
"My Jobs" manages the roles you've posted and the applicants to those specific roles. "Candidates" is the full directory of everyone registered on Meslova, including people who haven't applied to any of your jobs yet.
Can I filter applications by status?
Yes — both the per-job applicant list and the "All applications" page support filtering by status (applied, processing, hired, rejected), including a combined "Pending review" filter.
